=====
GATSBY❣️
=====
Jay Catsby — A wealthy, mysterious man who throws extravagant parties. He is deeply in love with Daisy and wants to recreate their past together.

Nick Carraway — The narrator of the novel. He moves to West Egg and becomes Catsby's neighbor. He is also Daisy's cousin.

Daisy Buchanan — Catsby's former love and Tom's wife. She is beautiful, charming, and wealthy, but often indecisive and careless.

Thomas "Tom" Buchanan — Daisy's husband. He is wealthy, arrogant, aggressive, and unfaithful to Daisy.
